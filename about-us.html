WHO WE ARE

A family story shaped by heritage, flavour and three decades of hospitality

Shozna began in 1996 with a vision inspired by family and culinary heritage. Named after Founder and Executive Chef Jamal “Jay” Ahmed’s sister, the restaurant first opened in Strood before finding its home in historic Rochester.

For 30 years, Chef Jay and his brother have welcomed guests with authentic Indian and Bangladeshi cuisine. Their dedication has made Shozna a trusted setting for family meals, celebrations and memorable evenings.

Jamal “Jay” Ahmed Founder & Executive Chef

THREE DECADES OF CULINARY EXCELLENCE

An Award-Winning Executive Chef

Chef Jamal “Jay” Ahmed transforms regional Indian and Bangladeshi traditions into refined dining experiences. His cooking honours generations of culinary knowledge while bringing imagination, balance and elegance to every plate.

After entering the restaurant trade in 1989, Chef Jay developed a deep understanding of spices, regional recipes and traditional preparation. In 2003, he reached the National Curry Chef Competition final as the South East finalist.

His dedication continued to earn recognition. In 2025, Shozna was named Authentic Restaurant of the Year for South East England at the Asian Curry Awards. The honour celebrated three decades of skilled cooking, gracious service and loyal community support.

Jamal “Jay” Ahmed Award-Winning Executive Chef

A TASTE OF THE SUBCONTINENT

Culinary Traditions Refined Across Generations

The cuisine of the Indian subcontinent is shaped by its landscapes, people and cultural traditions. Every region carries a distinct culinary identity, expressed through its spices, ingredients and methods of preparation.

Bangladesh and South India are celebrated for fish, fragrant rice and richly layered sauces. North Indian and Pakistani cooking often favours spiced meats, tandoor dishes, naan and chapatis. Moghul cuisine adds another level of refinement through aromatic spices and delicate sauces. It became known as the legendary “Food of Kings”.

Shozna brings these diverse traditions together with respect and imagination. Authentic techniques remain at the heart of the kitchen, while thoughtful presentation gives each dish a distinctive modern character.

Our menu offers familiar favourites alongside regional specialities. Guests can discover smoky tandoori lamb chops, lamb shank Chettinad, citrus-marinated nimbuwali jhinga and our signature Shozna naan.

Spices are used to create depth, aroma and balance, not simply heat. Each dish is prepared to offer an experience rich in the colours, flavours and hospitality of the East.

HERITAGE MEETS ELEGANCE

A Distinguished Setting in Historic Rochester

Shozna occupies a characterful building that was once the King’s Arms. Its historic surroundings provide a fitting home for a restaurant built upon culture and tradition.

Inside, sparkling chandeliers, Indian antiques and carved wooden mirrors create a sense of quiet grandeur. Modern furnishings and atmospheric lighting introduce a contemporary finish.

The setting feels elegant without losing its warmth. It creates a welcoming atmosphere for intimate dinners, family gatherings and life’s most meaningful celebrations.

OUR STORY CONTINUES

Thirty Years of Flavour, Family and Hospitality

Awards have marked important moments in Shozna’s journey, but guests remain at the heart of its story. Families have celebrated milestones here. Friends have reunited around its tables. Generations of diners have returned for the food and welcome they remember.

Thirty years after opening, Shozna remains guided by the same values: family, authenticity, culinary artistry and gracious hospitality.

Its story lives in recipes refined over time, a devoted team and every guest welcomed through the door. As Shozna enters its next chapter, it continues to honour the past while creating new memories in historic Rochester.
