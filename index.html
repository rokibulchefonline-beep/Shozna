MEET THE CHEF

Jamal “Jay” Ahmed

“Cooking is an expression of our culture, our passion, and the timeless tradition of Indian and Bangladeshi hospitality.”

Jay Ahmed moved from Birmingham to London at sixteen and began building his restaurant experience at an early age. In 1989, he became a partner in his first restaurant.

He established Shozna in Strood in 1996 and named the restaurant after his sister. Shozna later moved to its present home on Maidstone Road in Rochester in 2005.

Over the following years, Jay gradually developed the restaurant's dining spaces, bar and private function room. Shozna remains independently owned and operates from one location.

Jay continues to lead the kitchen and is regularly present in the restaurant, welcoming guests and overseeing the food.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

A few useful details before you join us. If there is anything else we can help with, our team is only a phone call away.

Is Shozna an Indian or Bangladeshi restaurant?

Shozna serves both Indian and Bangladeshi cuisine. Its menu combines Bengali specialities, regional Indian recipes, Mughal-inspired dishes, tandoori cooking, biryanis and familiar curry-house favourites.

Where is Shozna located?

Shozna is located at 153 Maidstone Road, Rochester, Kent, ME1 1RR. The restaurant is convenient for visitors from central Rochester, Strood, Chatham and surrounding Medway areas.

Does Shozna offer food delivery?

No. Shozna does not provide a delivery service. Online takeaway orders must be collected from the restaurant at 153 Maidstone Road, Rochester.

How long do collection orders take?

Collection orders are usually ready in around 30 minutes. Preparation times may be longer during busy evenings, weekends and public holidays. Your estimated collection time will be confirmed when ordering.

Can I order Shozna food online?

Yes. You can browse the collection menu and place your order online. Your meal will be cooked to order and collected directly from Shozna in Rochester.

Can I book a table online?

Yes. Tables can be booked online for evening dining from 5pm, seven days a week. Advance booking is recommended for Friday evenings, Saturdays and larger groups.

Does Shozna accept walk-in customers?

Walk-in guests are welcome when tables are available. Booking in advance is recommended during busy periods to avoid waiting.

Is Shozna suitable for wedding receptions?

The upstairs Shozna Dining Room can host wedding receptions and private celebrations for up to 60 diners. Bespoke menus, floral arrangements and audiovisual equipment can be discussed in advance.

Is Shozna suitable for corporate dinners?

Yes. The private dining room can accommodate corporate dinners, business functions and team celebrations. Audiovisual equipment is available on request.

Does Shozna serve halal food?

Please contact Shozna directly for current information about meat sourcing and halal preparation. This ensures you receive accurate guidance before booking or ordering.
