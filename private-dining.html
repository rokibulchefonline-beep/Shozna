HELP & INFORMATION

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the maximum capacity for private dining?

Our upstairs Private Dining Room comfortably seats up to 60 guests for a sit-down meal. For larger functions, exclusive full restaurant hire is available on request.

Is there a minimum spend or room hire fee?

Room hire fees or minimum spend requirements depend on the day of the week, timing, and guest numbers. Contact our event team at 01634 846 846 for a tailored quotation.

Can we bring our own celebration cake and decorations?

Yes! You are welcome to bring celebratory cakes, floral displays, balloons, and table decorations. Our team will store and serve your cake with our compliments.

Can dietary requirements such as Halal, Vegan, or Gluten-Free be accommodated?

Absolutely. All meats served at Shozna are 100% Halal certified. We have extensive vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free banquet options, and our chef can cater for any specific allergy.

Where can guests park nearby?

Convenient public parking is available on Union Street, just a short two-minute walk from our restaurant on Maidstone Road. On-street evening parking is also accessible nearby.
