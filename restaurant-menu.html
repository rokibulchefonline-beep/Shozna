BEFORE YOU VISIT

Frequently Asked Questions

Opening times, booking, dietary requirements and practical details for your evening at Shozna.

What time is the restaurant open?

Shozna serves dinner from 5 pm to 11 pm Sunday to Thursday, and 5 pm to midnight on Friday and Saturday. We are open on bank holidays.

Do I need to book a table?

Booking is recommended, particularly on Friday and Saturday evenings and for larger groups. You can book online or call 01634 847 847.

Is there a bar and a wine list?

Yes. The ground floor has a fully licensed bar serving cocktails, Indian and draught lagers, spirits and an extensive wine list running from house selections to Chablis, Châteauneuf-du-Pape and Champagne.

Do you cater for vegetarians, vegans and dietary requirements?

Yes. There is a full vegetarian section on the à la carte menu and most Indian and Bangladeshi dishes can be prepared vegan on request. Tell us when you book and our kitchen will advise on allergens.

Where can I park near the restaurant?

There is a public car park in Union Street, just a short walk from the restaurant on Maidstone Road and a few minutes from Rochester High Street.

Is Shozna suitable for private parties?

The upstairs Shozna Dining Room can host wedding receptions and private celebrations for up to 60 diners. Bespoke menus, floral arrangements and audiovisual equipment can be discussed in advance.
