BEFORE YOU BOOK

Private Dining Questions

What is the maximum capacity of the Upstairs Dining Room?

The upstairs private dining suite accommodates up to 60 seated diners comfortably with bespoke table arrangements for banquets, meetings, or celebrations.

How do I book or enquire about the private room?

We take private dining reservations exclusively by telephone to ensure bespoke arrangements. Call our private hire line directly on 01634 846 846 or 01634 847 847.

Are bespoke menus and AV equipment available?

Yes. Our executive chefs prepare multi-course bespoke menus tailored to dietary preferences, and we provide modern AV presentation screens and sound facilities upon request.

How do we book standard regular dining instead?

For standard dining and walk-ins, please visit our Downstairs Dining Space or book online instantly.
