FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Private Hire Information

What is the maximum dining capacity upstairs?

The Upstairs Dining Room accommodates up to 60 seated guests comfortably with custom table arrangements for dining banquets, parties, or formal meetings.

Can we customize our menu for the private room?

Yes. Our executive chef will work directly with you to curate multi-course bespoke tasting or banquet menus tailored to your guests' taste and dietary preferences.

Is audio-visual (AV) equipment available for presentations?

Yes, high-fidelity sound, microphone, and presentation AV equipment are available on request when booking your corporate or private function.

How do we book standard regular dining instead?

For standard dining and walk-ins, please visit our Downstairs Dining Space or book online instantly.
