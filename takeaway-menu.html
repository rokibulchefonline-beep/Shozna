COLLECTION QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering, collection times, prices and parking at Shozna.

Do you deliver?

Shozna does not deliver. Orders placed online are collected from 153 Maidstone Road, Rochester ME1 1RR, and are ready in around 30 minutes.

How long does a collection order take?

Everything is cooked to order and usually ready in around 30 minutes. On busy Friday and Saturday evenings it can take a little longer, and we will tell you when you order.

What time can I collect my order?

Collection is available at lunch from 12:00 PM to 1:30 PM, and every evening from 5:00 PM – until 11:00 PM Sunday to Thursday and midnight on Friday and Saturday. We are open on bank holidays.

Are takeaway prices the same as the restaurant menu?

No. Collection is priced separately and is lower than the à la carte menu served in our dining rooms. The prices on the takeaway and counter menus are collection prices.

Where can I park to collect my order?

There is a car park in Union Street, a short walk from the restaurant on Maidstone Road and a few minutes from Rochester High Street.
