BEFORE YOU VISIT

Downstairs Dine-In Questions

Do we need to make a reservation for lunch or dinner downstairs?

Walk-ins are always welcomed! During peak Friday and Saturday evenings, reservations are recommended to secure your preferred dining time without waiting.

What are the standard dine-in opening hours?

We serve Lunch from 12:00 PM to 1:30 PM daily, and Evening Dinner from 5:00 PM to 11:00 PM (11:30 PM on Friday and Saturday).

Can we order takeaway or home delivery from this restaurant?

Yes! You can explore our Takeaway Menu and order directly online for fast collection or local delivery.

What if we want a private room for an event or large party?

For private celebrations, business events, or parties up to 60 guests, please explore our Upstairs Private Dining Room and call 01634 846 846.
